# Domain glossary

- **Story Bible** — The stable visual and narrative identity shared by every generated beat.
- **Canon** — Facts that have become true in the story and must remain true after steering.
- **Story State** — The smallest current set of characters, place, important objects, unresolved goal, and canon needed to continue.
- **Beat** — One causal unit of story: setup, committed action, visible consequence, and a stable ending state.
- **Narration Segment** — One completed spoken sentence that becomes a single generated beat.
- **Rolling Continuation** — A beat generated automatically from the preceding beat's final frame while earlier footage is playing.
- **Steer** — A child's spoken or typed idea that updates the next continuation without pausing the visible story; stale unseen beats are discarded only when the guided replacement is ready to generate.
- **Guidance Branch** — A replacement chain generated from the final frame of the currently visible clip while that clip continues playing, then displayed at its natural ending boundary.
- **Visual Stop** — An explicit learner request to end the visual story, or a terminal budget/failure condition; speech interruption, topic changes, and completed clips never stop playback or rolling generation.
- **Scene Spec** — A compact description of the beat's narrative purpose, concrete actions, and ending state.
- **Continuity Key** — The identity of a story world whose recurring characters and environment must be preserved.
